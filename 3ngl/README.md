# 3NGL — English grammar tracker

**3NGL** helps you learn English grammar step by step, from beginner (A1) to mastery (C2), and keeps track of what you already know. Explanations are in **English** or **Bahasa Indonesia**.

👉 **Open the app:** https://verkno.github.io/L4NG/3ngl/

It's free, needs no account, and works offline once installed.

---

## What's inside

- **82 grammar topics** in six CEFR levels: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2.
- Every topic opens to show:
  - **What it is**: a short plain-language explanation.
  - **Pattern tables**: the forms you need, with the key words highlighted in red.
  - **Examples**: real English sentences (with Indonesian translations in ID mode).
  - **Tip**: the most common trap and how to avoid it.
  - **My notes**: your own notes and examples.
- **"Putting it all together"** at the end of each level: sentences that combine that level's grammar. Every part of each sentence is colour-coded, explained, and linked back to its topic.
- **Level estimate**: the app tells you which level you've reached and what you're working on now.
- **Common confusions**: short answers to the mix-ups learners make most often, such as past simple vs present perfect, for/since, will/going to, a/the, make/do, say/tell, its/it's and must/have to.
- **Verbs in each level**: the verbs used at that level, grouped by spelling rule (regular) and by pattern (irregular: cut-cut-cut, buy-bought-bought, come-came-come, go-went-gone), with the he/she, past and past participle forms.
- **Exam for each level**: multiple-choice questions picked at random from a bank of almost 1,000. Every exam tests every skill of the level at least once (minimum 10 questions) and is different each time. You see your score, your time, the right answers, and which skills to review. Your best score is saved.

## How to use it

1. Open a level and expand a topic.
2. Read the explanation and the tables, then mark the topic:
   - **New**: haven't started
   - **Learning**: working on it
   - **Known**: can use it confidently
3. Use **Search** to find a topic (e.g. *conditional*, *passive*, *since*). Search works in both languages.
4. Use the **New / Learning / Known** filters to see what's left to study.
5. Finish a level with its **"Putting it all together"** lesson.
6. Take the level's **Exam** to check yourself.

A level counts as **reached** when 80% of its topics are marked Known, in order (A1 before A2, and so on).

### Language

Switch between **EN** and **ID** at the top. In **ID** mode explanations, tables and buttons are in Bahasa Indonesia, and every example gets a translation. The English examples always stay English.

## Install it on your phone

1. Open https://verkno.github.io/L4NG/3ngl/ in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
2. Android: menu **⋮** → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**. iPhone: **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. It now opens from its own icon, full screen, and works **without internet**.

## Your progress

Your progress and notes are saved **on your own device**. Nobody else can see them, and there's no account.

To move progress between devices you have two options, both under **Sync & backup**:

- **Sync with GitHub (automatic):** if you have a GitHub account, create a token with only the **gist** permission (the app links to the right page), paste it in once on each device, and your progress stays in sync. It's stored in a **secret gist in your own GitHub account**.
- **Backup (manual):** copy the backup text on one device and paste it into **Restore** on the other.

Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes progress saved only on that device, so make a backup or use sync if it matters to you.
