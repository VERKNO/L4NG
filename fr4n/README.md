# FR4N — French grammar tracker

**FR4N** helps you learn French grammar step by step, from beginner (A1) to intermediate (B1), and keeps track of what you already know. Explanations are in **English** or **Bahasa Indonesia**.

👉 **Open the app:** https://verkno.github.io/L4NG/fr4n/

It's free, needs no account, and works offline once installed.

---

## What's inside

- **49 grammar topics** in three CEFR levels: A1, A2 and B1.
- Every topic opens to show:
  - **What it is**: a short plain-language explanation.
  - **Pattern tables**: the forms you need, with the key endings highlighted in red.
  - **Examples**: real French sentences with a translation in your interface language.
  - **Tip**: the most common trap and how to avoid it.
  - **My notes**: your own notes and examples.
- **"Putting it all together"** at the end of each level: sentences that combine that level's grammar. Every part of each sentence is colour-coded, explained, and linked back to its topic.
- **Level estimate**: the app tells you which level you've reached and what you're working on now.
- **Common confusions**: short answers to the mix-ups learners make most often, such as passé composé/imparfait, avoir/être, du/le, c'est/il est, qui/que, y/en, subjonctif or indicatif and tu/vous.
- **Verbs in each level**: the verbs used at that level, grouped into the three French verb groups (-er, -ir like finir, and the 3rd group), regular and irregular, with the je and nous present forms, the past participle and whether it takes être.
- **Exam for each level**: multiple-choice questions picked at random from a bank of over 570. Every exam tests every skill of the level at least once (minimum 10 questions) and is different each time. You see your score, your time, the right answers, and which skills to review. Your best score is saved.

## How to use it

1. Open a level and expand a topic.
2. Read the explanation and the tables, then mark the topic:
   - **New**: haven't started
   - **Learning**: working on it
   - **Known**: can use it confidently
3. Use **Search** to find a topic (e.g. *subjonctif*, *passé composé*, *en*). Search works in both languages.
4. Use the **New / Learning / Known** filters to see what's left to study.
5. Finish a level with its **"Putting it all together"** lesson.
6. Take the level's **Exam** to check yourself.

A level counts as **reached** when you **pass its exam (70% or more)**, or when 80% of its topics are marked Known, in order (A1 before A2, and so on). Passing doesn't mean you're finished: topics still marked Learning or New stay visible, so you can review them and make the level solid.

### Language

Switch between **EN** and **ID** at the top. Explanations, tables, buttons and translations of the examples follow your choice. The French examples always stay French.

## Install it on your phone

1. Open https://verkno.github.io/L4NG/fr4n/ in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
2. Android: menu **⋮** → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**. iPhone: **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. It now opens from its own icon, full screen, and works **without internet**.

## Your progress

Your progress and notes are saved **on your own device**. Nobody else can see them, and there's no account.

To move progress between devices you have two options, both under **Sync & backup**:

- **Sync with GitHub (automatic):** if you have a GitHub account, create a token with only the **gist** permission (the app links to the right page), paste it in once on each device, and your progress stays in sync. It's stored in a **secret gist in your own GitHub account**.
- **Backup (manual):** copy the backup text on one device and paste it into **Restore** on the other.

Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes progress saved only on that device, so make a backup or use sync if it matters to you.
